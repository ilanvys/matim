## Anchors (3)

- **A1 — 45 days is read as a position between thresholds, not as a quantity.**
  Clears 40+ (**מענק משפחה מוגדל**, **מענק הוצאות אישיות** — both paid daily from
  day 41, explicitly all-or-nothing, no pro-rata below the threshold) and 45+
  (**מענק כלכלת הבית מוגדל**, **שובר נופש**, **סיוע במעבר דירה**,
  **מענק משפחה מיוחדת**). Does **not** clear 50+ (סיוע נפשי, tuition support) or
  54 (מענק שנתי למפקדים). Naming two or more thresholds correctly on each side of
  45 earns this; a flat list of grants with no thresholds does not.
- **A2 — amounts are banded by מדרג פעילות.** א'+ / א' / ב' / ג' / ד' / ה',
  assigned by the IDF per unit and service type, confirmed at **1111 ext. 4 then
  1**. So no shekel figure is answerable without the tier. The published mapping
  is explicitly illustrative and not binding.
- **A3 — the tax credit is conditional in three ways.** Amendment 283 / Section
  39B: **combat days only** (ימי לוחם — non-combat service earns nothing),
  45 days lands in the **40-49 band = 0.75 credit points ≈ 2,178 NIS**, it is
  credited **in the tax year after the service year**, and the claim requires an
  **אישור לוחם** attached to טופס 101 — a general service confirmation is
  rejected. Two of these four earn the anchor.

## Anti-anchors — any one fails the anchor tier

- **Quotes a shekel grant amount without the tier.** The daily rates run 83 down
  to 21 NIS across מדרג for the same grant; a single number is wrong five ways out
  of six.
- **Claims 60 days of post-service dismissal protection.** At 45 days served it is
  **30** days under Section 41A(b). The 60-day tier needs 60+ days served, and its
  days 31-60 come from a collective-agreement extension order via ועדת פיקוח at
  the Ministry of Labour — not from the statute, and not from ועדת התעסוקה.
- **Says the tax credit is automatic, or applies to non-combat days.**
- **Tells the reservist to go claim these today.** The skill is explicit that most
  2026 grants are announced but not open — the full קרן הסיוע regulations are not
  published and the package is conditional on the 2026 budget law. The correct
  framing is *expected entitlement, watch `miluim.idf.il`*.

## Judge notes

The user asked what is due **beyond** the regular payment, so an answer that
explains תגמול מילואים is off-target however accurate. The question is the
grant/credit layer.

This is the case where 2026 figures move fastest — re-read the source before a run.
