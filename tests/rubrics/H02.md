## Anchors (3)

- **A1 — the form.** Names **טופס 355** (תביעה לתשלום דמי לידה ליולדת), or **356**
  where the birth grant is claimed with it. Not a generic "file a claim."
- **A2 — the likely cause, which is that no claim was filed.** Separates
  **מענק לידה** (automatic — the hospital reports the birth, bank details are given
  there) from **דמי לידה** (a claim the mother files herself). "A month and
  nothing arrived" is usually not a lost payment; it is an unfiled 355. An answer
  that treats both as automatic, or both as claimed, misses the question.
- **A3 — where to go.** The **אזור אישי** at `ps.btl.gov.il`, which auto-generates
  the form PDF, or **\*6050**. Credit for adding the qualifying period as the thing
  to check first: **10 of the last 14** or **15 of the last 22** months for the
  full 15 weeks; **6 of 14** or **10 of 22** for the reduced 8 weeks.

## Anti-anchors — any one fails the anchor tier

- **States a shekel amount the user will receive.** The base is the last 3 months
  ÷ 90 or the last 6 ÷ 180, whichever is higher, and the user gave no wage. The
  daily cap (1,752.33 NIS in 2026) is a ceiling, not an entitlement.
- **Says the employer files the maternity claim.** The mother does.
- **Says the hospital handles דמי לידה.** It handles מענק לידה only.
- **Sends the user to the labour court, or to an appeal, as a first step.** Nothing
  has been refused yet. Appeals belong only after a decision: ועדת ערר / טופס 7810
  within 60 days, and the regional labour court within 12 months.

## Judge notes

Amounts are the January 2026 circular and shift on 1 January, so an answer that
tells the user to verify at `btl.gov.il` or the אזור אישי is doing the right
thing, not hedging.

A month postpartum is the audience: a correct answer that opens with contribution
tables rather than "file 355, here" is answering a different question. Judge the
routing, not the completeness.
