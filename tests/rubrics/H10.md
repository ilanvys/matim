## Anchors (3)

- **A1 — the שירות צבאי section, as a section.** A distinct block — unit or unit
  type, role, rank, dates, 1-2 bullets on transferable skills — that Israeli tech
  HR scans, with **nothing classified**; when in doubt, role title and skill area
  only. This is the single largest divergence from the US and European formats the
  user is implicitly comparing against, so an answer without it has not answered
  "is there a local format".
- **A2 — the omissions, with the statute behind them.** Under
  **חוק שוויון ההזדמנויות בעבודה, התשמ"ח-1988**: no photo, no date of birth, no
  marital or parental status, no religion, no תעודת זהות. The part that separates
  a read answer from a remembered one is **place of residence** — a protected
  ground since **תיקון 22 (2014)** — so the CV keeps the **city** and drops the
  **street address**, and drops country-of-origin markers too. Naming the law plus
  the city-not-street rule earns the anchor.
- **A3 — Hebrew-specific mechanics.** At least two of: **RTL** layout; phone as
  `050-123-4567` with no country code (the English version uses `+972-…`);
  **Gregorian** dates, `01/2023 - היום`; modern tech Hebrew — **"פיתחתי",
  "הובלתי", "שיפרתי"** rather than formal or biblical register; gender-neutral
  phrasing where possible, matching the user's own where not, **never assumed**;
  unit names in Hebrew (**"מודיעין - יחידה 8200"**).

## Anti-anchors — any one fails the anchor tier

- **Recommends a photo.** Omitted by default even in Hebrew; included only on
  explicit request **and** for a non-tech role (acting, hospitality), and
  **never** on the English version. "Israeli CVs usually have a photo" is a
  European convention imported wholesale.
- **Recommends age, date of birth, marital status, number of children,
  תעודת זהות, or a full street address.**
- **Treats the military section as optional or datable-away** for an experienced
  candidate. It stays for Israeli citizens.
- **Ships US-template furniture**: an Objective statement, "references available
  on request", or two-plus pages for a mid-level candidate. The rule is one page
  for junior and mid-level, two only at 10+ years.
- **Writes bullets in the first person.** Bullets open with the verb, not with
  "אני".

## Judge notes

The only `dev` case in the first ten. The register may be technical, but nothing
else relaxes — G2 and T3 apply unchanged.

describes the skeleton and the rules is complete; one that jumps to generating a
document without the inputs Step 1 asks for is answering a question that was not
asked. **Asking for the target role, work history and military details before
