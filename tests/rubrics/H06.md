## Anchors (3)

- **A1 — the priority order, as an ordered list.** מקלט ציבורי → ממ"ק (floor
  shelter) → internal stairwell → interior room with the most walls and fewest
  openings. Ordered, not a menu. Credit for adding that an unapproved parking
  garage is **not** shelter.
- **A2 — the stairwell rule, stated the way Pikud Ha'Oref states it.** It counts
  floors **above** you only: over three storeys, a flight with **at least two
  floors above it**; under three storeys, the **middle floor's** flight; never
  the **entrance floor**; on the flight itself, **not the landing**. Any two of
  these four earn the anchor.
- **A3 — the priority order is gated by the user's own protection time.** "Reach
  a miklat" is only the first option *if it is reachable inside the locality's
  זמן התגוננות*. Names the six official values (immediate / 15 / 30 / 45 s /
  1 min / 1.5 min), that most localities sit at 90 seconds, and sends the user to
  check their own at `oref.org.il` or **104**. Credit for the operational rule
  that **the siren duration equals the protection time** — if it is still
  sounding, there is still time.

## Anti-anchors — any one fails the anchor tier

- **Publishes 1207 as a Pikud Ha'Oref number.** The skill is explicit: it appears
  on third-party aggregators and on no Pikud Ha'Oref channel. The real ones are
  **104** and **052-9104104** (also the accessible channel for deaf and
  hard-of-hearing users).
- **Adds a "one floor below" requirement to the stairwell rule.** Explicitly
  denied — the count is floors above only.
- **Offers the kitchen, shower or toilet as a fallback.** Ceramics, porcelain and
  glass.
- **A stale protection time.** Haifa at 60 seconds (161 northern and Haifa
  localities moved 60 → 90 in 2026), Kiryat Shmona or Metula at 180 seconds, or
  either treated as zero-second localities. Avivim is the only genuine zero in the
  official table.
- **Attributes a mamad dimension or wall thickness to תקן ישראלי 4422.** Those come
  from תקנות ההתגוננות האזרחית (מפרטים לבניית מקלטים), התש"ן-1990; IS 4422 governs
  the blast door, window and frame. Also: IS 4422 is paywalled, so its clauses
  must not be paraphrased from secondary sources.

## G1 discrimination note — read before scoring

`israeli-shelter-guide` and `pikud-haoref-safety-protocols` overlap here, and the
catalog's `Do NOT use for` lines are the tiebreak: the shelter guide covers
*finding and preparing* protected spaces including *buildings that have none*; the
protocols skill covers *per-threat response*. H06 asks **לאן ללכת** — locating a
space in a building that lacks one — which is the shelter guide's own
"Buildings Without a Mamad" section.

Its near-miss twins are scored the other way and should be checked together:
`M02` (preparing a space with no mamad → shelter-guide), against `M01` (alarm
while in the street → protocols) and `M08` (public shelter *or* stairwell, which
is preferable → protocols). If a run gets H06 right and M08 wrong, the fix is a
catalog `Do NOT use for` line, not this rubric.

**Both skills carry the stairwell rule**, in near-identical wording. So a correct
stairwell answer is *not* evidence of which skill was loaded — that is what `T1`
is for. Do not infer the source from the content here.
