## Anchors (3)

- **A1 — the right order, from the right body.** **צו ירושה** — not צו קיום צוואה,
  which is the with-a-will path — filed with the **הרשם לענייני ירושה** online at
  `inheritance.justice.gov.il`. Credit for adding that the Registrar is the
  default forum and that Family Court is reached only through the closed list in
  **סעיף 67א** (an objection, the State as a party, the AG, the Public Trustee, or
  the Registrar's own transfer) — *"the will looks defective" is not a transfer
  ground*, and a minor heir commonly is.
- **A2 — the split turns on whether the mother survives, and the answer
  establishes that before dividing anything.** Under **סעיף 11**, a surviving
  spouse takes the household chattels including the family car, then **one-half**
  of the rest where there are children, with the children splitting the other half
  equally; with no surviving spouse the children take the whole estate in equal
  shares. An answer that divides the apartment three ways without asking about the
  mother fails this anchor even if the arithmetic is internally consistent.
- **A3 — one live procedural fact.** Any of: the objection period the Registrar
  sets, which by law cannot be under two weeks and in practice is **14 days**;
  orders are now **digital only** and transmitted directly to banks, the Land
  Registry and insurers; the fee is **597 NIS listed, 507 online** (online filing
  costs 85% of the listed fee, and a represented applicant must file online) plus
  a separate **66 NIS publication fee**.

## Anti-anchors — any one fails the anchor tier

- **Says there is inheritance or estate tax in Israel.** There is none — the
  Estate Tax Law 1949 was repealed for deaths after 31 March 1981. The exposure
  is **מס שבח** when an inherited asset is later sold, which is a different
  question and outside the skill.
- **Applies the apartment proviso to the children.** The rule that a spouse of
  3+ years living in the apartment takes the deceased's **entire** share in it
  sits in the **two-thirds** branch (siblings/grandparents), not the children
  branch. Misplacing it hands the mother the whole flat when the law gives her
  half of the rest.
- **Routes them to Family Court, or to a lawyer, as the default first step.** The
  Registrar is the default; the skill's own referral triggers are an objection, a
  business or foreign assets or an insolvent estate, a minor needing a guardian,
  or doubted validity.
- **Treats the father's pension or life insurance as part of the estate.**
  **סעיף 147**: amounts payable on death under an insurance contract, pension fund
  or provident fund pass to the named **מוטבים** and are outside the estate — and
  neither the will nor the succession order redirects them.

## Judge notes

`שני אחים` is ambiguous — the asker's two brothers (making three children), or
the deceased's two brothers (which would put the estate in the siblings branch and
change everything). A good answer notices and asks. Treat a stated assumption,
clearly labelled, as equivalent to asking; treat a silent assumption as an A2 miss.

Bereavement is the audience. An answer that opens with the fee table rather than
"you need a צו ירושה, here is where" is answering correctly and unhelpfully — note
it, do not fail it.
