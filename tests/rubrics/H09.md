## Anchors (3)

- **A1 — named no-broker channels, not "look online".** At least two of: the Yad2
  **ללא תיווך** filter, Facebook groups where owners post directly (**מבעלים**),
  Homeless (English-friendly, popular with olim), WinWin, or approaching a
  building's **ועד בית** directly.
- **A2 — the legal half of "בלי תיווך", which is where the money is.** Under
  **section 25ט** — non-waivable — a residential tenant **cannot be required to
  pay a fee to a broker who acted on the landlord's behalf**; the fee is owed by
  whoever hired the broker. And under the 2024 ethics regulations (in force
  9 March 2025) a broker who did not get the client to sign a **הסכם תיווך** in
  advance **is not entitled to a fee at all, even if they showed the apartment**.
  Either limb earns the anchor. This is what separates a real answer from a list
  of websites.
- **A3 — Tel Aviv speed is the actual constraint.** Listings can disappear within
  hours; check at least twice daily, set **התראה** alerts, respond within minutes,
  and have the document pack ready in advance (תעודת זהות, 3 תלושי שכר, בank
  statements, **ערב**) so an offer can be made on the spot. Demand peaks June-
  September. Credit for noting a new oleh without a local ערב can often
  substitute an **ערבות בנקאית**.

## Anti-anchors — any one fails the anchor tier

- **Says the tenant owes the broker's fee as a matter of course.** Directly
  contradicts 25ט. The typical fee is one month's rent **+ 18% VAT** — but only
  from the party that hired the broker.
- **Quotes the price table as current market fact.** The skill's own figures rest
  on Q3 2025 data and it says early-2026 supply tightened and asking rents rose
  further — *treat these as a floor and budget above them*. A confident
  "3 rooms in Tel Aviv costs X" without that caveat fails.
- **Miscounts rooms.** Israeli **חדרים** includes the salon: a דירת 3 חדרים is
  2 bedrooms plus a living room, and a sun balcony counts as 0.5. A model
  answering in US convention will size the search wrong.
- **Recommends a שטר חוב / promissory note or a signed blank guarantee** as a
  routine way to look attractive to a landlord without a broker.
