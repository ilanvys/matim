## Anchors (3)

- **A1 — the right paragraph, quoted as a ceiling.** **הורה יחיד** under
  **תקנה 2(א)(10)**, a national ceiling of **up to 20%** — and the answer must say
  the council chooses where inside that ceiling it sits **and may sit at zero**.
  "You are entitled to 20%" is the failure this anchor exists to catch; the
  regulation is national, the rate is not.
- **A2 — only one discount applies.** **תקנה 17(א)**: where several fit, the
  holder gets the single highest, and no discount goes to a second holder of the
  same property. So the answer should send her to compare הורה יחיד against the
  **income test at תקנה 2(א)(8)**, whose thresholds rise with household size and
  which can exceed 20%. Naming the income route as the alternative worth checking
  earns this even without the regulation number.
- **A3 — how it is actually claimed.** Applied for at **the municipality**, not at
  a national body; **renewed annually**; documents are single-parent recognition
  (Bituach Leumi or a court order), the child's birth certificate, and income
  documentation.

## Anti-anchors — any one fails the anchor tier

- **States a definite percentage she will receive.** The ceiling is not the rate.
- **Offers a national large-family (משפחה ברוכת ילדים) or student rate.** The
  Arnona Discount Regulations contain **no** such paragraph. Any such discount is
  a municipal bylaw creation with no national rate to quote.
- **Treats the baby as producing the entitlement.** The entitlement is the
  single-parent status under the Single-Parent Families Law 5752-1992; the child
  is evidence for it, and the alternative 2(a)(10) limb (a child in conscript or
  national service, age ≤ 21) does not apply here.
- **Counts child allowance (קצבת ילדים) as income** in the 2(a)(8) test. It is
  expressly excluded, along with disabled-child benefit and reserve-service
  special payments.

## Judge notes

"גרה לבד עם תינוק" states a living arrangement, not a legal status. A strong
answer asks whether she is recognised as הורה יחיד — a separated-but-not-divorced
parent often is not — before promising the paragraph applies. Asking is not
hedging here; it is the eligibility test.

Ceilings and thresholds reset annually. The percentage ceiling is stable; the
income thresholds are not.
