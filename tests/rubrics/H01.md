## Anchors (3)

- **A1 — Form 161, and the two parts.** Names טופס 161, and that Part A is the
  employer's notice while Part B is where *the employee* elects what happens to
  each severance component. Credit for noting the form was unified in January
  2024, replacing the standalone 161א.
- **A2 — the default is continuity, not payout.** Says that if Part B is not
  returned, the default is **רצף קצבה** (pension continuity), not an automatic
  cash payout — *"This is the opposite of the pre-2024 rule."* This is the anchor
  that most cleanly separates a loaded skill from a remembered one.
- **A3 — the cost of taking the cash.** Names היוון קצבה: each shekel of
  tax-exempt severance withdrawn **within 32 years of legal retirement age**
  reduces the lifetime קצבה-מזכה exempt pool by **1.35×** (the מקדם). Full credit
  requires the 32-year window, since below it the penalty is zero. Naming the three
  options (רצף זכויות / ביטול פיצויים / פריסת פיצויים, up to 6 tax years) also
  satisfies A3 if the 1.35 appears.

## Anti-anchors — any one fails the anchor tier

- **Quotes a deadline for returning Part B.** The skill explicitly refuses to:
  *"commonly cited as about 10 business days … but is NOT verified in this skill:
  read the window off the Part A form … and do not quote a number to the user."*
  A model that states "10 days" is answering from memory inside a skill that told
  it not to.
- **Recommends withdrawing the severance** without the היוון קצבה consequence.
- **Says severance is simply tax-free**, with no ceiling. It is exempt to
  13,750 NIS per year of service; above that, marginal rate.
- **Treats Section 14 as binary.** The skill lists four real-world sub-cases and
  the 6% vs 8.33% gap the employer may still owe at final salary.

## Judge notes

"6 years" is a hook, not just tenure: a good answer separates the **pension**
(portable, stays in the fund, keeps accruing with the next employer) from the
**severance** (the decision) and, if a קרן השתלמות exists, notes that 6 years is
exactly when gains become tax-free. Answering only about the pension misses the
question's actual money.

Bounded by the skill's own legal notice — informational, and a licensed
**יועץ פנסיוני** (fee-only, fiduciary) rather than a סוכן or משווק for anything
over ~100,000 NIS of severance.
