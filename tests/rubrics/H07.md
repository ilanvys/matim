## Anchors (3)

- **A1 — the week as an ordered sequence, not a list of errands.** Day 1-2
  **משרד הפנים** with the תעודת עולה, passport and photos; Day 1-3 **bank account**
  and **קופת חולים**; Day 1-7 **ביטוח לאומי**, an Israeli SIM, and a MyGov account;
  Week 1-2 **אולפן**. Four of these five in roughly this order earns the anchor.
- **A2 — a dependency inside the sequence, not just its order.** Any one of: the
  **תעודת זהות follows automatically** from the משרד הפנים registration and is not
  issued at the airport (the airport issues the **תעודת עולה**, which is the ID
  until then); **MyGov needs the Israeli SIM** for SMS verification, so the phone
  comes first; the **bank account must exist** for the sal klita instalments to
  land; a couple opens a **joint** account.
- **A3 — what the airport itself does.** The **משרד הקליטה desk at Ben Gurion**
  issues the תעודת עולה, the first sal klita payment and a starter SIM on arrival.

## Anti-anchors — any one fails the anchor tier

- **Sends a French oleh to Nefesh B'Nefesh.** NBN serves olim from the **USA,
  Canada and the UK**. This is the one country-specific fact the query supplies,
  and it is the sharpest signal in the case: an answer that offers NBN is
  answering a generic aliyah question and did not read the source. French olim go
  through the Jewish Agency and Misrad HaKlita's own French-language channels.
- **Says the תעודת זהות is issued at the airport.**
- **States a sal klita amount** without the family-status track and the 24-month
  presence test the ministry applies.
- **Puts ulpan registration before משרד הפנים**, or MyGov before the SIM.

## Judge notes

**A known source conflict — do not fail either wording.** `SKILL.md` Step 3 calls
the first sal klita payment a prepaid card *"with a larger cash component during
strikes, chagim and emergencies"*; `references/aliyah-timeline-guide.md` line 12
says *"First payment in cash"*. The upstream files disagree. Accept either; note
it in `notes`. It is worth reporting to skills-il, and it is a good demonstration
of why anchors get written against the files rather than from memory.

France is Hague-apostille territory, so document authentication is the ordinary
apostille route — but that is pre-arrival and the user asked about week one.
Raising it briefly is fine; leading with it is answering the wrong question.
